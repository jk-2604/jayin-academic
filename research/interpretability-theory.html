Interpretability & Theory of Deep Learning

Attribution methods for understanding neural networks and language models.

Research focus

This work studies how attribution techniques can identify the inputs and internal representations that drive model predictions, with an emphasis on methods that remain useful for sequential models and LLMs.

Methods

  • Integrated Gradients, Manifold Integrated Gradients, and Guided Integrated Gradients.
  • Extensions to sequential models and language models.
  • Granger causality and Randomized Path Integrals for additional interpretability signals.
