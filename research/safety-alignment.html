Alignment & Safety in T2I and T2V Models

Safety-guided flow matching for text-to-image and text-to-video diffusion.

Current direction

This research explores a neurosymbolic safety-alignment approach using scene graphs and a safety-potential-guided rectified flow-matching formulation in CLIP embedding space.

Evaluation

  • Benchmarking against TRCE, CURE, SAEUron, and DoCo.
  • Evaluating harmful-content reduction on the DETONATE dataset.
  • Studying methods that improve safety without retraining the full generative model.
