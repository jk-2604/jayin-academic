Undergraduate Thesis

Generative models for unsupervised speech time-scale modification.

Overview

This thesis studies unsupervised speech time-scale modification using generative models. The work compares VAEs, GANs, diffusion models, and flow-matching approaches for changing speaking rate while preserving intelligibility and speaker characteristics.

Research focus

  • Implemented DiffATSM, ScalerGAN, TSM-Net, and Neural ATSM from scratch in PyTorch.
  • Compared 10 models across three methodology classes and 19 experiments.
  • Jointly supervised by Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh (SPIRE Lab, IISc) and Prof. Niteesh Sahni (SNIoE).
  • Awarded 2nd Prize for Best Undergraduate Thesis.
