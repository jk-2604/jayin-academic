Current Research

Safety-guided flow matching for T2I/T2V diffusion

AI Institute of South Carolina (Dr. Amit Sheth, Dr. Amitava Das). Developing a safety-potential-guided rectified flow-matching formulation in CLIP embedding space to reduce harmful generations, benchmarked on DETONATE against TRCE, CURE, SAEUron, and DoCo.

Post-hoc trustworthiness repair in LLMs

Deep Representation Learning Lab, IISc (Dr. Prathosh A. P.). Combining EK-FAC curvature estimation with targeted gradient ascent to mitigate bias, unethical outputs, and toxicity in fine-tuned models.

My undergraduate thesis on generative models for unsupervised speech time-scale modification, under the joint supervision of Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh and Prof. Niteesh Sahni, won 2nd Prize for Best UG Thesis. I document this work as detailed technical notes on my Research page.
