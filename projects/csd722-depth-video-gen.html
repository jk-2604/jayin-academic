CSD722: Depth-Conditioned Video Generation using ControlNet & AnimateDiff

Jan – May 2026 · Supervisors: Dr. Sumit Shekhar & Dr. Saurabh Shigwan

Extended ControlNet to text-to-video diffusion models using AnimateDiff and Motion LoRA.

  • Modified the architecture to prevent generated videos from ignoring depth-map conditioning.
  • Added auxiliary supervision losses to stabilise training.
  • Conducted ablations on prompts, depth conditioning, and text guidance.
