Full-Waveform Inversion for Seismic Velocity Prediction

Ongoing · Supervisors: Naveen Gupta & Divakar Vashisth

Extending a latent-space-translation framework for subsurface forward and inverse problems using diffusion priors and flow matching.

  • Working on optimal-transport-based misfit functions as an alternative to L2 loss.
  • Studying cycle-skipping on Marmousi and Overthrust benchmarks.
  • Surveying diffusion and flow-matching generative priors for Bayesian FWI.
