Time Series and Sentiment Analysis in US Financial Markets

2024–2025 · Supervisor: Dr. Charu Sharma, Mathematics Department, SNU

Modelled 2-, 5-, and 10-year bond yields using seven years of daily data across 31 financial indicators.

  • Applied PCA and Mutual Information to identify 10 indicators explaining 95% of variance.
  • Used rolling windows to study market regimes and time-series patterns.
  • Combined statistical analysis with deep learning models for financial trend prediction.
