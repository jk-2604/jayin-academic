Preprocessing Pipeline and Application of CNNs for Surface Electromyography (sEMG) Signal Classification

2024 · Supervisor: Dr. Shilpi Modi, Institute of Nuclear Medicine and Allied Sciences (INMAS), DRDO

Developed a CNN ResNet model for classifying sEMG stress measurements, improving classification accuracy from 91% to 97.98%.

  • Built a preprocessing pipeline for raw surface electromyography (sEMG) signals prior to model training.
  • Tuned CNN hyperparameters to improve generalisation and classification performance.
  • Explored sequence models as an extension for generalising beyond the CNN baseline.
